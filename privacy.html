MEHdictation Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

MEHdictation is a local-first Windows clinical dictation application. Speech recognition and dictated clinical content are processed locally on the user's own computer rather than being sent to a cloud transcription service.

In short: microphone audio, dictated text, Recent activity, application names and window titles are not uploaded for cloud processing or Private Sync. Optional Private Sync can synchronise Dictionary entries, Templates and aggregate usage statistics between the user's devices using end-to-end encrypted data.

Information MEHdictation accesses

MEHdictation accesses the microphone when the user starts dictation. It may also process text created by the user and locally stored application data including dictation history, Dictionary entries, Templates, preferences, hotkey settings and aggregate usage statistics.

Local speech processing

Speech recognition in the Microsoft Store edition is performed locally on the Windows device using the bundled local speech-recognition runtime and model. Dictated audio and resulting clinical text are not sent by MEHdictation to a remote transcription or language-model service.

Optional Private Sync

Private Sync is optional and must be enabled by the user. When enabled, MEHdictation can synchronise Dictionary entries, Templates and aggregate usage statistics between the user's MEHdictation installations. Sync payloads are encrypted on the user's device before transmission and are designed so that the sync service does not receive the plaintext contents.

Private Sync does not include microphone audio, dictated text, Recent activity/history content, application names or window titles. Aggregate statistics may include totals such as words dictated, dictation counts and speaking-rate statistics rather than the content of individual dictations.

Storage

Dictation history, settings and other application data are stored locally on the user's device. When Private Sync is enabled, encrypted synchronisation data for Dictionary entries, Templates and aggregate statistics is also stored by the MEHdictation sync service so that it can be retrieved by the user's other devices.

Sharing and third parties

MEHdictation does not sell dictated content or user data and does not disclose dictated audio or clinical text to advertisers or data brokers. The optional Private Sync service is used only to provide the user-requested synchronisation function. Distribution through the Microsoft Store is also subject to Microsoft's own Store, account, acquisition and diagnostic processes, which are governed by Microsoft's privacy terms rather than this policy.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

MEHdictation does not include advertising and does not use third-party advertising trackers. Aggregate usage statistics are kept locally and, only when Private Sync is enabled, may be included in the user's encrypted sync data for cross-device statistics.

Clinical and sensitive information

MEHdictation may be used in clinical environments. Users remain responsible for using the software in accordance with their employer's policies, professional duties and applicable information-governance requirements. Dictionary entries and Templates chosen for Private Sync should not contain patient-identifiable information unless the user and their organisation are satisfied that doing so is permitted.

User control

Microphone access is controlled through Windows permissions and by starting or stopping dictation. Locally stored history, Dictionary entries and Templates can be managed from within the application. Private Sync is optional and can be left disabled. Users can stop using MEHdictation and remove it from Windows at any time.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if MEHdictation's data handling changes materially.

Support and privacy questions

For support or privacy questions, use the MEHdictation support page.
