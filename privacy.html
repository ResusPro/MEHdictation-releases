MEHdictation Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

MEHdictation is a local-first Windows clinical dictation application. Its design principle is that speech processing and dictated clinical content remain on the user's own computer rather than being sent to a cloud transcription service.

In short: MEHdictation does not upload dictated audio or dictated clinical text for cloud transcription, does not require an MEHdictation account, and does not sell user data.

Information MEHdictation accesses

MEHdictation accesses the microphone when the user starts dictation. It may also process text created by the user, together with locally stored application data such as dictation history, dictionary entries, templates, preferences and hotkey settings.

How information is processed

Speech recognition in the Microsoft Store edition is performed locally on the Windows device using the bundled local speech-recognition runtime and model. Dictated audio and resulting clinical text are not sent by MEHdictation to a remote transcription or language-model service.

Storage

Application data such as dictation history, dictionary entries, templates and settings is stored locally on the user's device. Users can delete individual items within the application where those controls are provided, and can remove application data from the device when no longer required.

Sharing and third parties

MEHdictation does not sell dictated content or user data and does not disclose dictated audio or clinical text to advertisers or data brokers. Distribution through the Microsoft Store is subject to Microsoft's own Store, account, acquisition and diagnostic processes, which are governed by Microsoft's privacy terms rather than this policy.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

MEHdictation does not include advertising and does not use third-party advertising trackers. The application does not require an MEHdictation cloud account.

Clinical and sensitive information

MEHdictation may be used to dictate health information because that is central to its intended function. Users remain responsible for using the software in accordance with their employer's policies, professional duties and applicable information-governance requirements. MEHdictation is designed to minimise disclosure by keeping dictation processing local.

User control

Microphone access is controlled by the user through Windows permissions and by starting or stopping dictation. Locally stored history, dictionary entries and templates can be managed from within the application. Users can stop using MEHdictation and remove it from Windows at any time.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if MEHdictation's data handling changes materially, including if future versions introduce any optional online processing.

Support and privacy questions

For support or privacy questions, use the MEHdictation support page.
