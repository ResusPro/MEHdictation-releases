MEHdictation

Clinical dictation,
without the cloud.

MEHdictation is a simple Windows dictation tool designed to make clinical documentation quicker while keeping speech processing and clinical data on your own computer.

Local

Runs on your computer

Speech recognition and dictation processing happen locally on the Windows computer running MEHdictation.

Private

Your clinical data stays local

Dictated audio and clinical text are not sent to a cloud transcription service for processing.

Simple

Built to get out of the way

Start dictating, get the words you need, and move on. No cloud account, online patient record or remote transcription workflow is required.

Local-first by design. No cloud transcription. No audio upload. No dictated patient data sent away for processing.
